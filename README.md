# TheRoamingKin

**TheRoamingKin** is a real-world lifestyle RPG where your daily habits become character progression.

Instead of only tracking steps, calories, or time spent learning, this app transforms your real-life actions into **experience points, achievements, and RPG stats**.

Go to the gym? Your character gains **Strength**.

Spend time at the library? Your character gains **Intelligence**.

Walk in the park and explore nature? Your character gains **Agility, Dexterity, and Healing**.

> Improve your real life, while leveling up your fantasy self.

---

# Why This App Exists

Most habit apps track numbers:

* Steps walked
* Calories burned
* Minutes studied
* Time exercised

But numbers often feel boring and disconnected.

**TheRoamingKin** turns those numbers into something meaningful:

* Character growth
* Levels
* Achievements
* Story progression
* Unlockables

# Features

## Location-Based Achievements

Using device location, the app can detect visits to places such as:

* Gym
* Library
* Park
* Outdoor locations

## Health Integration

With Apple Health / HealthKit:

* Calories burned
* Cardio activity
* Steps walked
* Exercise duration

## Image Classification

Users can optionally take photos during activities.

Examples:

* Book at library
* Sunset in park
* Tree
* Animal
* Nature scene

The app can classify the image and reward bonus achievements.

## Character Progression

Your hero levels up based on your real-world consistency.

Unlock:

* Levels
* Titles
* Gear
* Stats
* Badges
